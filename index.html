About Me

Over 30 years in Design and Construction, with an early involvement in Graphics and Printing.

My experience, though for the most part concentrated in the Caribbean, is quite wide ranging, from small timber structures to complex Civic buildings and high end luxury villas.

My responsibilities have included Interior Design, Architectural Design and Project Management.

The vast majority of my experience is in a fast paced environment, working among and coordinating with professionals from across the globe.
